The Tropical Rainforest.
Explore its information, technology, principles of life, and mysteries to be discovered.

The universe is information; what we know is simply the retrieval. Here, you will learn about the technology used to retrieve what we have so far about tropical rainforest civilizations: LIDAR, carbon dating, DNA analysis, photogrammetry, ground radar, palaeoecology, etc., but most importantly, when studying human civilizations, the layer of our own abstract contextualization. Propose new technology and methods, your own informational process, and abstract realizations.
